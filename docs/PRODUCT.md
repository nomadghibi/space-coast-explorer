# Product

A mobile-first tourism product for curated local exploration, tours, media, AI guidance, itineraries, merchants, analytics, and billing over time.

M1 positioning: visitors should understand within seconds that Space Coast Explorer helps them discover self-guided experiences, local stories, hidden places, beaches, space history, food, and more.

## Freemium Positioning

Free access must be useful enough for a visitor to complete a selected route without paying. For the Cocoa Village pilot, free access includes the full ordered route, clickable mapped stops, short landmark summaries, directions, manual completion, progress, distance, elapsed time, and basic read-more context.

Premium should sell depth and convenience, not basic viability. Premium unlocks should focus on full landmark stories, audio narration, offline access, food and shop pairings, custom itineraries, and cruise timing help. Checkout, subscription state, entitlements, and payment-provider integration remain M8 scope; earlier milestones may show premium preview surfaces only.
